# Character artwork

**Tiny Swords (old CC0 release), by Pixel Frog**

- Creator and download page: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords
- Download selected: **TS_old version_CC0 Licensed**, dated January 3, 2024 on the creator's download page.
- Archive supplied by the creator: `Tiny Swords.zip`, containing `Tiny Swords (Update 010)`.
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded November 7, 2026. Original paths, dimensions, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Only Pawn, Warrior, and Archer PNG sheets in four colors are included. These PNG files are unmodified. Infantry variants share Warrior art; crossbowmen share Archer art. Healers share Pawn art, and mounted units combine Warrior riders with a Canvas horse silhouette. Small role markers and additional faction hues are drawn by the game. Siege engines retain Canvas artwork.

The newer Tiny Swords Free Pack and paid Enemy/Warfare packs are **not** included. Their current licensing terms differ from this explicitly labeled CC0 archive. Do not assume newer downloads have the same license.
