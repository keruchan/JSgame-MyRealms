# Building artwork

**Tiny Swords, old CC0 release, by Pixel Frog**

- Creator and original download: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords
- Selected archive: **TS_old version_CC0 Licensed** (`Tiny Swords.zip`, containing `Tiny Swords (Update 010)`).
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- These assets come from the same creator-provided archive used for the character sprites. `manifest.json` records the archive checksum, original paths, PNG checksums, dimensions, and draw-time crop rectangles.

The 18 PNGs are unmodified originals: four faction colors each for Castle, House, Tower, and Wood Tower, plus Goblin House and Active Gold Mine. Only the explicitly labeled old CC0 archive is used; newer Free Pack and paid packs are not included.

King of the Conqueror's Realm composes these original sprites in Canvas to represent its eleven building types. Farms add crop fields and fencing, lumber camps add logs, archery ranges add targets, barracks add a shield emblem, stables add a horse pen, workshops add a gear and workbench, and Stone Keeps combine a castle with extra towers. Faction flags and extra faction hues are drawn at runtime. The same artwork is used for map buildings, placement previews, selection portraits, and Help entries.
