King of the Conqueror's Realm

Kingdoms at War · Real-Time Skirmish

Found a settlement, harvest resources, train an army, fortify your borders, and conquer rival kingdoms. The enemy builds, expands, raids and fights back — in one continuous campaign.

🌾 Economy: directed gathering, repairs, tools research
🏰 Construction: resource houses, training buildings, towers
⚔️ Warfare: attack-move, counters, rally points, groups
🚩 Map control: capture crossings for gold and food; rivals contest them

Tip: Select your Town Hall to recruit workers and research upgrades. Right-click a tree or ore with workers selected to gather; right-click a damaged friendly building to repair. Select a military building and right-click ground to set a rally point.
